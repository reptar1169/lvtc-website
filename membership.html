Paying your dues

Scan the code with your phone, or tap the button if you are already reading this on one. Either way it opens Venmo at @lindavistatowncouncil. Include your name and address in the note so we can add you to the roll.

Paying the student rate? Put “student” in the note and show a current school ID at any meeting.

Open Venmo

Prefer not to use Venmo? Bring cash or a check to any meeting, or get in touch and we will sort it out.
