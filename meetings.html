Meetings and minutes

The council meets twice a month and both meetings are open. You do not have to be a member to attend — though the general meeting is in person only, and the board meeting is on Zoom only.

The public record

Every meeting, and where its minutes stand

This lists every meeting the council has held since January 2023 — not only the ones with a document on file — so it is always clear what has been recorded and what is still coming.

How minutes work. The secretary drafts minutes after each meeting and the council approves them at the following one, so a meeting from the last few weeks will usually show as expected rather than posted. If something older is missing and you need it, ask us — we can usually send a draft.
